# Custody hazards in prior work

The first column identifies the study or implementation audit and the amendment rows that record its findings. The ledger includes hazards that recur in several studies, along with their distinct failure modes. Older exact-delivery work is conditional under D01. Ordinary admitted I/O and exact mutation receipts still have obligations.
