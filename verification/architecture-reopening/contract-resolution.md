# Logkeeper protocol decisions after the tension review

2026-11-23

Each row gives the boundary that protects the required behavior and the remaining decision, if a caller or operator would see a changed guarantee.

| Tension | Protocol decision | Remaining evidence or decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One regional cell and one ordered history | Use stable bounded journal groups under one regional operating boundary. Keep ordinary append, same-group writer replacement and checkpoint publication in the installed group's order. Compute pools and discovery scale independently. True transfer needs a funded exact source seal and successor installation. | Bring the RTDB target and integration to this boundary. Set Q01 thresholds and verify scaling under repair and placement skew. [Architecture comparison](comparison.md) |
| Fresh observations and exact retry | Keep an explicit distinction between a new latest observation, a caller-named basis, and recovery of an original protected invocation or mutation. Latest uses its own freshness interval and exact local protection. Exact recovery uses original identity and retained evidence. Width one preserves sequential admission. The selective candidate uses a packed canonical checkpoint and the same exact capture and delivery order. The verified current grant sets the width, which replacements inherit unless an authenticated change is chosen. | Verify Bijection's caller contracts before changing ambiguous response behavior. Qualify larger windows for independent callers and refine the actual chosen proof and checkpoint wire binding. [Caller inventory](../92-read-contracts/report.md) · [Selective recovery proof](../102-selective-ordered-recovery/report.md) · [Grant policy proof](../103-grant-read-policy/report.md) |
| Serving through uncertainty about an AZ | Open a locally verified serving cut on its exact surviving quorum and inputs. Until the first physical loss is independently established, acknowledge only with protection against all first-loss cases and preserve previous protection through withdrawal. Recover confirmed loss and renewal in chosen order across voter replacement. Renew with verified current voter and data coverage and a fair ready turn. | Fund enough independent durable capacity for the unclassified-loss write path. Carry the interval across placements of the same deployment incarnation. Refine witness and pending-withdrawal recovery and authenticated exact provider effects. One-copy acknowledgment under uncertainty would change the public protection promise. [Diagnosis comparison](../99-degraded-evidence/report.md) · [Protection model](../105-service-scope-protection/report.md) · [Ordered recovery](../106-ordered-fault-interval/report.md) · [Protection handoff](../107-protection-handoff/report.md) |
| Fast logical retirement and delayed physical effects | Keep three observable facts distinct. The ordered authority has closed new admission, the original effect authorities have settled or effectively stopped admitted work, and all physical custody in scope has verified disposition. The caller's requested receipt determines which fact it waits for. | Confirm whether a caller expects a strict stop before its retirement response. That stronger promise depends on exact enforcement at the affected effect authorities. [Standing custody](../31-standing-custody/report.md) · [Provider closure](../40-provider-io-closure/report.md) |
| Bounded storage and exact history | Keep finite replay debt, independently bounded retained history, and reserved checkpoint and settlement workspaces. Normal watches reopen from a certified snapshot after their cursor falls below the retained floor. A required exact historical feed gets a separate configured horizon and custody. | Qualify recovery and checkpoint throughput and confirm caller retention requirements. [Retention proof](../03-retention/report.md) · [Exact event feed](../17-exact-feed/report.md) |
| Shared utilization, moves and guarantees | Reserve the physical peak at each required pool, including old pins, staged copies and repairs. Protect hard guarantees with credits that unrelated irrevocable work cannot use. Compare cross-pool preparation policies off the foreground path with the same capacity and fault budgets. | Choose placement and isolation tiers using admission and repair measurements. [Capacity comparison](comparison.md#executable-comparisons) · [Isolation proof](../09-isolation/report.md) |
| Fast reuse and crash-safe accounting | Order refund and subsequent charge at their original pool owner. Keep exact recovery through every eligible selector and voter change. Let historical receipts reconcile only their earlier result. Spend only from the current applied ledger and charge the overlap of protected redo and replacement images. | Verify the actual compact checkpoint, exact scoped command, and media layout. [Settlement redo](../98-settlement-redo/report.md) · [Ranked recovery](../100-ranked-pool-refund/report.md) |

The general proofs and finite counterexamples establish the model boundaries.
The unresolved caller, fault policy and qualification items determine where a different external guarantee would be needed.
