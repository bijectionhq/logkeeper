# Asynchronous ordered-group refinement

[AsyncGroup](Logkeeper/AsyncGroup.lean) models one installed voter roster with any number of overlapping rounds, any finite execution length, and arbitrary message scheduling. The model makes no fairness or termination claim. Loss, duplication, delay, and reorder act on complete RPC messages or unfinished exchanges. The sender and receiver advance separately within a live exchange. Leadership replacement closes the old round locally and leaves its dispatched work and any later durable effects in the model.

[AsyncPrefix](Logkeeper/AsyncPrefix.lean) maps stored promises, the latest accept, and historical votes and reports to `DurableAcceptor.State`. Immutable prepare quorums, selected reports, issued histories, and historical durable accepts give the `PrefixAgreement.Ledger`. Its `LedgerStep` describes opening a ballot, issuing a prefix, adding a vote, and stuttering. The latest media and the history of evidence have different lifetimes. Cancellation and reopen do not erase historical evidence.

## Actions and proof boundaries

| Shipped path | Modeled effect and reference |
| --- | --- |
| [`Group::prepare`, `Group::confirm`](../../src/ordered/replication.rs) | Start a fresh ballot from a configured peer set. Dispatch each prepare or accept separately. Each successful poll consumes the corresponding promise or vote once while the round is in the right phase. Timeout, refusal, loss, cancellation, and replacement can leave already-dispatched tasks running. Finishing prepare archives the first quorum's replies, and the successful `chooseBase` fold implements `PrefixAgreement.WinnerDominates`. The vote gate binds voter, ballot, image, mirrored slots, and read-back, then applies `PrefixTrace.ConfiguredMajority`. |
| [`Group::choose_stamped`, `recover`, `compact`, `repair_seed`](../../src/ordered/replication.rs) | The prepare result enables replay and at most one proposal. A new decision extends the recovered seed by one frame, while retry, recovery, and compaction preserve the logical history. The nonempty recovery path confirms before returning. A sealed seed uses `OrderedCohort.may_extend` and the export uses `OrderedCohort.matches_chosen_seal`. The confirmed export holds an exact historical choice. |
| [`RemoteVoter::{open,response,prepare,accept}`, `serve_voter`](../../src/ordered/replication/transport.rs) | Separate wire actions model connect, magic, operation/status, group/voter, ballot round and nonce, flags, length and body, digests, slot generations, flush, and timeout. The same read/write action can recur for multiple fields. A completed call enables the server worker. A completed reply enables a client poll. The local replica path can deliver the same typed message without traversing the wire. Individual buffering, byte-level partial reads, and socket close are abstracted to delays, completed fields, or loss. |
| [`DurableVoter::{prepare,accept}`, `Inner::{prepare,accept,persist,read_back_slot}`](../../src/ordered/replication/disk.rs) | Start a serialized worker from an admitted call. Advance the first slot through body write, tail clearing, header write and sync. The first stable image supplies `DurableAcceptor.PrepareAdmitted` or `AcceptAtPromise` effects. The second slot and requested read-backs must finish before a successful reply leaves the voter. A live worker can outlast the caller's future. |
| [`DurableVoter::{recover,open,initialize_repaired}`](../../src/ordered/replication/disk.rs) | A crash can discard a pending first image or leave it eligible for selection. Reopen advances through slot reads, authentication, selection and stabilization. Adopting a complete new candidate performs the saved transition with no reply. Repaired media is unavailable until exact seed installation; its reserved ballot uses a synthetic empty prepare, and each completed install supplies one bootstrap vote with the exact seed history. |

`step_projects_ledger` and `step_refines_acceptor` prove the projections for every modeled action from a reachable state. `io_evidence` derives the provenance of in-flight and counted vote replies from the durable effect that produced them. `exact_certificate_is_chosen` then obtains `PrefixAgreement.Chosen` from the counted replies and the exact quorum. `choice_survives_step`, `one_choice_per_position`, `chosen_prefixes_compatible` and `later_recovery_preserves_chosen_prefix` apply to every reachable model state. `later_recovery_keeps_exported_history` composes a confirmed export with a named successor initialized from that artifact and a strict majority of its bootstrap votes. The [reachability witness](Logkeeper/AsyncGroupWitness.lean) runs a three-voter cohort, crashes the first voter after it writes the alternate header, reopens the candidate without a reply, and has a replacement select and confirm that minority accept with a different quorum.

The local [PL11 Verus area](../verus/prefix_agreement/README.md) ties its decision laws to the reference. The following Rust-to-model boundaries still require a proof.

- The scheduler and authenticated transport present one stable voter per future. A dropped future cannot count a reply. The model proves a closed round rejects a later count and protects old work by ballot, but the correspondence from Tokio polling, dropped futures, detached blocking tasks, and RPC timeouts is an inspected boundary.
- Ballot UUID generation is fresh within the cohort and avoids the reserved bootstrap value. A ballot issues one exact image. The image's bytes, scope digest, durable identity, and full logical history keep their meaning through decoding, replay, and compaction. Prepare payloads are represented by their immutable saved snapshots. Neither the raw Promise payload to snapshot projection nor the full logical image projection is proved mechanically here.
- A serialized disk worker cannot acknowledge before the second synced image and requested read-back. Reopen can adopt a prior image or stabilize one complete pending image; it cannot regress past the first established effect. The model groups tail zeroing and assigns the abstract effect at first stabilization. Selection after sync errors, torn sectors, warm cache, and the repaired-media reservation marker requires the separate filesystem and crash refinement.
- The model accepts an authorized repair artifact at the successor boundary. Reading the reducer's exact seal, placement and predecessor checks, normalizing base bytes, paid reservation, selecting a partially installed base, and publishing a single authoritative successor are Rust-to-model boundaries. The transfer theorem begins with a confirmed source export and an exact installed majority. It does not prove authority uniqueness across competing repair operations.
- Native randomized tests check the projected events via `PrefixTrace.step`. That executable checker does not consume individual wire cursors, disk steps, or failed operations. No theorem establishes that each emitted native trace has a full `AsyncGroup.Step` witness.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run the Lean controls and the native trace generator.

```sh
python3 verification/lean/run_async_group_controls.py
python3 verification/lean/run_prefix_traces.py --seed 20261124 --repetitions 4
```

The runner retains the seed's schedule, traces and checker diagnostics under `verification/lean/.results`. Each repetition schedules prepare-request loss, accept-request loss, loss of a durable vote's reply, duplicated prepare and accept requests, coordinator cancellation after a durable accept, and reordering an old accept past a newer quorum. It varies roster order and delays, and alternates whether the voter reopens from disk. The baseline fixtures also cover compaction and sealed repair. The seed reproduces the schedule parameters; Tokio ordering and ballot UUIDs can vary by run.

Three definition mutations must make the first Lean error land at a named law. Weakening the exact floor check fails `native_accept_requires_exact_promise`, counting raw rows fails `certificate_requires_distinct_voters`, and keeping the older recovery candidate fails `recovery_pair_keeps_rank`. The native trace checker separately rejects four trace substitutions at the offending event.
