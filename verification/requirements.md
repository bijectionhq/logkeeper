# Logkeeper requirements and mechanisms

Draft for product review · 2026-11-22

RTDB resolves transactions, reads back committed records before applying them, and authorizes trim from published checkpoints.
Logkeeper orders opaque records within one stream per deployment incarnation.
The durability target is one AZ loss within a three-AZ region.
Recovery and commit latency targets need workload-specific budgets.

## Owner decisions · 2026-11-23

The owner accepted these decisions for RTDB Multi with Logkeeper.
They take precedence over conflicting rows below until those rows are rewritten.

| ID | Decision | Effect on this design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Reads have no exact lost-reply recovery. A retried read is a new admission at the same logical basis, matching RTDB's read-basis decision. Mutations keep exact retry through their original receipts. | S04's exact invocation delivery and the read reissue, nonce floor, delivery book and selective window mechanisms (Studies 80–91 and 101–104) leave the baseline. An orphaned owned-read handle expires through its lease or hold. |
| D02 | Erasure reaches journal-retained history through key custody, never by revoking holds. Following RTDB's R7 design, an erasure orders a clean successor checkpoint at a journal cut and rotates the journal payload key generation at that cut. The commit arms revocation of the old generation, and destroying it within RTDB's deadline makes the retained old ciphertext unreadable. | Logkeeper digests, receipts, checkpoints and archives cover ciphertext only. Recovery after the commit starts from the clean root, and its suffix uses only the new generation. A view or hold on old-generation ciphertext gets `rtdb/erased-under-view` after the deadline. Removing undecryptable old ciphertext is later provider cleanup. |
| D03 | Acknowledging on a single surviving copy while AZ loss is unclassified stays forbidden. | S02 and the protection interval of Studies 105–107 stand. The unclassified-loss write path must be funded or wait. |
| D04 | The baseline has no exact historical feed. Watches restart from a certified snapshot after retention passes their cursor. | The exact feed of Study 17 is a possible separate product with its own horizon and custody. |
| D05 | The portable guarantees apply only to installed journals. Legacy and directory deployments keep their documented semantics and migrate by cutover to a fresh deployment. | The bypass audit must show that no directory path serves an installed deployment. Legacy roots without digests are outside portable publication. |
| D06 | Q01 budgets are set from measurement of the implemented system under representative workloads. | Healthy and one-AZ-down commit p99, writer replacement time, checkpoint and trim throughput above ingest, and voter repair completion remain stated premises until measured. |
